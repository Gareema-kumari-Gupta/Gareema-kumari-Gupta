## About Me

I lead a development team building a **fitness tracker platform**, where I architect backend systems designed for reliability and scale. Alongside this, I serve as **Vice Chair of my Computing Club** and have led internship teams end-to-end — from system design to deployment.

I care about building things that hold up under real use: clean APIs, sensible data models, and infrastructure that doesn't fall over. Currently deepening my knowledge of **backend architecture and system design patterns** for large-scale applications.

**Currently:** Leading backend architecture for a fitness tracker platform
**Open to:** Backend systems, Python applications, and computer vision projects with real-world impact
**Looking for guidance on:** Production deployment strategies and backend optimization at scale
**Ask me about:** Backend development, team leadership, computer vision, or Python (2+ years)
